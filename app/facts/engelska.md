# Engelska
Grammatik, verb, ordförråd och fraser som du behöver i engelskan, med svenska förklaringar.

## Verben be och have
| Person | be (vara) | have (ha) | Kort form |
|---|---|---|---|
| I | am | have | I'm, I've |
| you | are | have | you're, you've |
| he / she / it | is | has | he's, she's, it's |
| we | are | have | we're, we've |
| they | are | have | they're, they've |
Nekande: *I am not, he isn't, they aren't, she hasn't, we haven't.* Fråga: *Are you ready? Has she got a dog?*

## Tempus
| Tempus | Används för | Exempel |
|---|---|---|
| Present simple | vanor och fakta | I **play** football. She **plays** tennis. |
| Present continuous | det som pågår nu | I **am playing** now. |
| Past simple | avslutat i förr | I **played** yesterday. |
| Past continuous | pågick vid en viss tid i förr | I **was playing** when you called. |
| Present perfect | har hänt och har betydelse nu | I **have played** since 2020. |
| Future | framtid, planer och beslut | I **will** call you. I **am going to** visit Anna. |
= Present simple | lägg till -s med he, she, it: he plays. Nekande med don't/doesn't: she doesn't play.
= Past simple | regelbundna verb får -ed: walked, played. Nekande: did not + grundform.
= Present perfect | have/has + particip: I have seen it, she has gone.
> *Since* använder du med en startpunkt (since 2019), *for* med en tidsperiod (for three years).

## Frågor och nekande
| Frågeord | Betyder |
|---|---|
| what | vad |
| who | vem |
| where | var, vart |
| when | när |
| why | varför |
| how | hur |
| which | vilken |
| whose | vems |
= Present simple | Do you like pizza? Does she live here? (do/does + grundform)
= Past simple | Did you see the film? (did + grundform)
= Med be | Are you tired? Is he at home? (vänd på ordningen)
= Slutfråga | You like music, **don't you**? She is late, **isn't she**?
> Ordföljd i en fråga: *frågeord + hjälpverb + subjekt + verb.* Exempel: **Where do you live?**

## Substantiv och artiklar
= a / an | **a** före konsonantljud (a dog), **an** före vokalljud (an apple)
= the | bestämd form, för något specifikt (the dog on the road)
= Plural | oftast -s (dogs), -es efter s, x, ch, sh (boxes), -ies efter konsonant + y (babies)
| Oregelbunden plural | Exempel |
|---|---|
| man → men | woman → women |
| child → children | person → people |
| foot → feet | tooth → teeth |
| mouse → mice | sheep → sheep |
**Räkningsbara** ord går att räkna (one apple, two apples): använd *many*, *a few*, *few*. **Oräkningsbara** går inte att räkna (water, money, music): använd *much*, *a little*, *little*. *Some* används i positiva satser och *any* i frågor och nekande: *I have some money. Do you have any?*

## Adjektiv, adverb och jämförelser
| Typ | Regel | Exempel |
|---|---|---|
| Kort adjektiv | -er / -est | tall – taller – the tallest |
| Långt adjektiv | more / most | beautiful – more beautiful – the most beautiful |
| Slutar på -y | -ier / -iest | happy – happier – the happiest |
| Oregelbundna | helt nya former | good – better – best, bad – worse – worst |
| Oregelbundna | helt nya former | much – more – most, little – less – least |
Adverb säger *hur*: de bildas ofta med **-ly** (slow → slowly, quick → quickly). Några är oregelbundna: *good → well, fast → fast, hard → hard.*
> Jämförelse med *as … as*: *She is as tall as her brother.* Jämförelse med *than*: *She is taller than her brother.*

## Pronomen
| Subjekt | Objekt | Possessivt (före ord) | Possessivt (själv) | Reflexivt |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| I | me | my | mine | myself |
| you | you | your | yours | yourself |
| he | him | his | his | himself |
| she | her | her | hers | herself |
| it | it | its | – | itself |
| we | us | our | ours | ourselves |
| they | them | their | theirs | themselves |
> *Its* (dess) har ingen apostrof. *It's* betyder *it is* eller *it has*. Samma sak med *your* (din) och *you're* (du är).

## Hjälpverb och villkor
| Verb | Betyder | Exempel |
|---|---|---|
| can / could | kan / kunde | I can swim. |
| may / might | kanske, får | It might rain. |
| must | måste, säkert | You must stop. |
| should | borde | You should sleep. |
| will / would | ska / skulle | I will help. |
| have to | måste (regel) | I have to go. |
Efter hjälpverb kommer grundform **utan** to: *I can swim.*
### Villkorssatser (if)
| Typ | Form | Exempel |
|---|---|---|
| 0: alltid | if + presens, presens | If you heat ice, it melts. |
| 1: möjligt | if + presens, will + grundform | If it rains, we will stay home. |
| 2: osannolikt | if + past simple, would + grundform | If I were rich, I would travel. |
| 3: oförändrat | if + past perfect, would have + particip | If I had known, I would have come. |
> I engelskan skriver man *if I **were***, inte *was*, när det är ett önskemål.

## Passiv och indirekt tal
= Passiv | be + particip: The book **was written** by Selma Lagerlöf. (Boken skrevs av Selma Lagerlöf.)
= Indirekt tal | presens blir dåtid: "I am tired" → She said (that) she **was** tired.
= Indirekt fråga | ingen frågeordning: He asked where **I lived**.

## Skriva, ord och fraser
| Situation | Fras |
|---|---|
| Hälsa | Hello! Good morning. Nice to meet you. |
| Tacka | Thank you very much. You're welcome. |
| Be om hjälp | Excuse me. Could you help me, please? |
| Be om ursäkt | I'm sorry. Sorry I'm late. |
| Mejl (formellt) | Dear Mr Smith, … Yours sincerely, … |
| Mejl (vänner) | Hi Anna! … Best wishes, … |
**Länkord** gör texten sammanhängande: *and* (och), *but* (men), *because* (eftersom), *however* (men, dock), *therefore* (därför), *although* (fast), *first, then, finally*.
